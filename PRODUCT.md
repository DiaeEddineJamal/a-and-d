# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends opening a shared arcade library to choose a game quickly, then moving into either local play or code-based private online rooms.

## Product Purpose

Lmogolyan Arcade is the front door for a growing collection of independently runnable multiplayer games. It should make the two live titles feel collectible and desirable while keeping the path from browsing to playing immediate.

## Brand Personality

Playful, crafted, kinetic. The experience should feel like an exceptionally preserved arcade catalogue crossed with hand-inked theatrical game packaging.

## Anti-references

No generic gaming-dashboard UI, glassmorphism, violet neon, overcrowded collage, fake cultural motifs, or imagery associated with Mongolia. Avoid character faces on functional Pong equipment. Do not reproduce copyrighted characters, logos, or exact compositions from references.

## Design Principles

1. Make every playable title feel like a physical artefact worth picking up.
2. Use animation to communicate selection and momentum, not as constant decoration.
3. Preserve the aged print character while allowing strategic, confident color.
4. Keep the route to local or private online play obvious at a glance.
5. Use original imagery with personality and no cultural interpretation of the invented names.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion fallbacks, and labels that do not rely on color alone.
